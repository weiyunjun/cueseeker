# Playback feedback collapses to one centred anchor and drops its hardcoded capsule and shadow

Human-owned documents:

- `docs/human/requirements/20260908-UI-Playback Feedback Single Anchor And Style.md`

## Why

Playback feedback had three anchors — the centre of the picture and the two vertical mid-edges — so the same class of message landed in three different places and the eye had to find it each time. The centre anchor sat over the part of the frame the viewer is actually watching, and the capsule itself was a 60-point full-radius pill with a 30-point icon and 20-point semibold text, which reads as a button rather than a transient message even though `UI Application Rules.md` says feedback takes no input. None of those numbers came from a token: the component's `Sizes` section in `UI Design System.md` is empty, so every value lived only in the view. The owner asked for one anchor and for the style to be expressible in the design system. The anchor was first built at the top-leading corner, the mainstream player convention; after seeing it running the owner moved it to the centre of the stage and had the shadow removed. Both revisions are in the requirement and in the follow-up below.

## What changed

- `PlaybackFeedbackPosition` is gone, and with it the `position` argument on every `PlaybackFeedback`. All twenty-odd call sites construct the same shape now; direction is carried by the icon that was already there (`chevron.left` / `chevron.right`), not by where the capsule lands.
- `PlaybackFeedbackOverlay` places one capsule at the centre of the stage, in both axes. Nothing in its position depends on the chrome, so the capsule does not move when the Player UI hides, fullscreen toggles, or an overlay opens over it.
- The capsule is `--height-playback-feedback` tall with `--radius-playback-feedback` corners, `--spacing-4` horizontal padding and `--spacing-3` between icon and label; the icon is `--size-playback-feedback-icon`, the label `--font-size-playback-feedback` at `--font-weight-control`, both `--color-player-text-primary`. The four playback-feedback tokens are new and sit beside the speed menu's own in `PlaybackChromeMetrics`, which is where the player's component-scoped values already live; the two spacings are the generic ones. The icon's own drop shadow is gone; nothing inside the capsule casts one, as `UI Design System.md` requires. The surface is the speed menu's — `--color-player-menu-bg` under `.ultraThinMaterial` behind a `WorkbenchControlMetrics.hairlineWidth` border — minus its shadow; the follow-up below says why.
- The label is monospaced-digit and capped at `--width-player-topbar-title-max` with tail truncation, so a long Shiori list name cannot run across the picture. The capsule takes its ideal width: without `fixedSize` the stage proposes the whole window, the label's ceiling becomes its width, and every capsule renders as wide as the longest one can be — that was caught in the first E2E pass and fixed before the geometry checks below.
- Motion is `ToastCenter.motion`, the existing Toast Motion constant, replacing a local `easeOut(0.14)`. Only the capsule's presence animates; its content swaps in place, so a held-down seek key redraws the counter without the capsule fading under it. The entering scale grows from the centre, which is now also the anchor.
- The feedback expiry now clears both seek accumulators instead of choosing by position. In every reachable path this is the behaviour that was already there: a forward seek zeroes the rewind accumulator on the way in and vice versa, and every absolute seek — which is what result, bookmark and video navigation perform — zeroes both. The one sequence that differs is a rewind followed by a feedback that performs no seek at all, such as `No Result`, followed by another rewind: the counter used to resume from the earlier total across the unrelated message and now restarts. That is an artifact of keying the reset off the message's position rather than off the seek, and the requirement's "keep the accumulation as it is" is about the accumulation itself.
- `PlaybackNormalShortcutTests` loses three `position` expectations. The icon and text expectations in the same tests are untouched and still carry the value.

## Verification

Debug in-app E2E controller, main window 1445×821, Light appearance, English, one video opened from the first Recent Videos card. Feedback was triggered by the documented playback keys — space, the arrows, `q`, `p`, `d`, and `c` inside Subtitle Focus — with a 0.3 s settle before each capture, since the capsule fades in over `--duration-toast-motion` and its own life is under a second. Capsule geometry was measured out of the captures rather than read by eye, wherever the surroundings let a scan separate the capsule from the picture.

- Idle, with no feedback pending: no capsule.
- `Pause`, `+ 5s`, `+ 20s`, `100%`, `Subtitles Off`, `Cue Text Copied`, `Next Library Video`: every one in the same place, centred.
- `Cue Text Copied` over the Subtitle Focus panel, the one surface plain enough to measure the rounded corners cleanly, sits at (723.2, 410.5) against a stage centre of (722.5, 410.5) and measures 209.5 × 52.0 points: centred within a point, and the required 52-point height. The same message measured 163.0 × 40.0 before the enlargement, so the capsule grew 30% taller and 28.5% wider. On the video frames the capsule was confirmed visually; the centring is one `ZStack` alignment shared by every case, with no per-surface branch.
- Probing the light panel outward from all four edges of the capsule — 4, 8, 12, 24 and 40 points out — returns the surface's own luminance to within 0.05% in every direction: no shadow, and in particular none above the capsule. That probe was run against the 40-point capsule; the enlargement touched no part of the surface.
- With Subtitle Focus open, `c` shows `Cue Text Copied` centred over the cue list and above the overlay, so the copy feedback that the layer ordering exists for is visible, and it is nowhere near the overlay's top bar.

`swift test --filter CueSeekerAppTests`: 793 tests, 8 to 12 issues depending on the run, in three suites that have nothing to do with feedback. `DebugE2EControllerTests` and `LibraryFolderRemovalTests` pass in isolation, which is the load-dependent family the earlier records describe. `CueGroupRefreshScopeIntegrationTests` still fails `showCueGroupInvalidationIsScopedToMountedShiori` and `shioriTableStartsAtTopAfterPanelRemount` in isolation — the same two cases, on the same two expectations, that `2026-09-08-playback-topbars-share-one-layout.md` records as failing on that commit's parent. Every playback suite passes: `PlaybackNormalShortcutTests`, `PlaybackStoreTests`, `PlaybackShioriOverlayTests`, `PlaybackSubtitleFocusKeyboardActionTests`, and `WorkbenchControlMetricsTests`.

## Follow-up: the owner's two revisions

Both came from looking at the shipped commit, and both are in the requirement before the code moved.

- **The shadow was a grey cloud.** `--shadow-player-overlay` is `0 12px 24px rgba(0,0,0,0.42)` — a scrim tuned to float over black video, where the design system itself notes it is nearly invisible. The requirement had put the feedback layer above the Playback Overlay, so the near-white Subtitle Focus panel became one of its ordinary backgrounds, and there the same scrim reads as a smudge. Measured on that panel, the plume darkened the surface by 33% just below the capsule, was still 18% at 24 points out and only faded below 2% at 56 points — longer than the 40-point capsule is tall — and it leaked 15% *above* the capsule as well. Two things compound the token: the CSS blur radius was copied straight into SwiftUI's `.shadow(radius:)`, which is roughly a Gaussian sigma rather than a CSS blur, so it rendered about twice the token's size; and CSS's negative spread, which `--shadow-popover` gained in `20260906-UI-Popover Shadow Casting Source And Layered Recipe.md` precisely to stop the leak above a panel, has no SwiftUI equivalent and was never applied here — that requirement's §4.5 explicitly left playback feedback out of its scope. The owner chose to drop the shadow rather than calibrate it: the capsule is a 55%-black chip, high-contrast over video and over the light panel alike, and `UI Design System.md` already assigns the separation to the container's border. None of these three values changed in the first commit; the corner anchor and the smaller capsule only made them conspicuous.
- **The anchor moved to the centre.** The known cost, raised before the change and accepted: a centred capsule re-centres when the label's width changes, so an accumulating counter crossing a digit boundary — `+ 5s` to `+ 20s` to `+ 100s` — pushes both edges outward. Tabular figures keep the width steady within a digit count but cannot prevent that. The top-leading anchor was what made the left edge immovable.
- **The capsule was too small, and grew about a third.** Height, icon, label, radius and padding scale together, so the width follows the label. The interesting part is where the new numbers land: `--spacing-4` and `--spacing-3` are genuine reuse, but 52, 24, 18 and 16 have no existing token whose name means anything here — 52 exists only as the bottom controls row's height and 18 only as the Workbench section title's size, and borrowing either would assert a relationship that does not exist. The player's chrome is already tokenized component by component (`--height-player-capsule-button`, `--size-player-circle-button-icon`, `--radius-player-speed-menu`, `--font-size-player-speed-menu-item`), so four playback-feedback tokens follow that pattern instead of inventing a shared scale. The requirement's "no new tokens" line was dropped for the same reason.

## Known limits

The first Recent Videos card was reached with a coordinate read off a capture. The card coordinates in `docs/engineering/08_end_to_end_testing.md` §13 and §16 predate the Workbench topbar and sidebar changes of the last few days and no longer point at the controls they name; the sidebar item centres are still derivable from the tokens and were derived, but the Recent grid's are not. The deviation is recorded here rather than made silently, and the gate the coordinates belong to was not run.

Dark appearance and the Simplified and Traditional Chinese strings were not captured — switching either means changing the owner's settings from inside the App. The label is a single line of localized text in a self-sizing capsule with a 520-point ceiling, so the risk is confined to a longer translation truncating earlier.

The `Save to Shiori` capsule at its truncation ceiling was not captured: it needs a list whose name is long enough to reach 520 points, and none in the owner's data is.

`UI Design System.md` does not yet carry the four new playback-feedback tokens, and still describes the component with the generic popover tokens, an empty `Sizes` section, a `Shadow` the capsule no longer casts, and a three-valued `Position` on `Playback Feedback Overlay Layout`, and still places the layer below the Playback Overlay and the Command Panel where the App has always placed it above. Per the owner's instruction the design documents are updated after this round of UI tuning is accepted, so that gap is expected until then.
